🌍 From the studio

Open your game in the studio and press Share hub, then Share this game. Tick It is a utility app for a tool. You get a play link and a QR code, and Post to the hub puts it here.

Open the studio

📡 Bit to Bit

No internet needed. Open Share on the Utilities row of both PocketBits (press Down on the home screen), pick a game, app, photo or clip and the other Bit. It lands on their home screen.

📝 Straight to the form

Post a play link, a .bitkit file, photos or clips with a short GitHub form (a free account). Posts from new people show up after a quick look.

Open the form ↗

🎮 On your PocketBit

  1. Press ⬇ .bitkit on a game or app.
  2. Copy it to games/ on the Bit's memory card.
  3. It is on the home screen next time.
