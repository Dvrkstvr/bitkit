Cutscenes

          

          

            How cutscenes work
            
A cutscene is a list of steps that play in order: dialog, camera pans, walks, fades. While it plays, enemies and the player stop, nothing takes damage, and black bars slide in. START skips it.

            
Three ways to start one:

            
    
              
  1. When a level starts: pick it under Plays.
    2. 
              
  2. A zone on the map: press Place a trigger zone, then click on the map.
    3. 
              
  3. From code: cutscene("name"). on("cutscene_end", fn) tells you when it is over.
    4. 
            

            
Positions are in tiles. The Map tab shows the tile under the mouse.
