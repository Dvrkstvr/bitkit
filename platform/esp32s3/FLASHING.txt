PocketBit firmware (BitKit runtime on an ESP32-S3-WROOM-1-N16R8)

Flash it over the USB port (hold BOOT while plugging in if the board doesn't show up):

  pip install esptool
  esptool.py --chip esp32s3 -b 460800 write_flash @flash_args

storage.bin is the internal flash disk: the built-in games, the apps and the home screen. Flashing it again
replaces your saves on the internal flash (saves on an SD card are not touched). To update only the firmware:

  esptool.py --chip esp32s3 -b 460800 write_flash 0x10000 pocketbit.bin

Serial console (115200, the same USB port): logs, frame times every 5 seconds, and the commands
  time <unix seconds>    set the clock (idle games count the time you were away)
  lua <code>             run Lua in the game that is playing
  home                   go to the home screen

Benchmark: benchmark/pocketbit.bin is a firmware that measures every built-in game at boot (no screen, sound or
radio needed) and prints a table of CPU cycles per frame on the serial console. Flash everything above once, then:

  esptool.py --chip esp32s3 -b 460800 write_flash 0x10000 benchmark/pocketbit.bin

and open the serial monitor (115200). Flash pocketbit.bin at 0x10000 again to go back.
